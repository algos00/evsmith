EVSmith

Say what your Pokémon must survive, beat and outspeed. EVSmith builds the 66-point spread that does it with the fewest points.

How to use EVSmith

EVSmith finds the cheapest Stat Point spread (66 points in total, at most 32 per stat) that does everything you ask of your Pokémon: survive certain attacks, get certain KOs, outspeed or stay slower than certain Pokémon.

  1. Your Pokémon. Pick the species, ability, item and nature. “Auto” tries every nature and keeps the best. Points you type in the “Fix some of your points” boxes are never changed; empty boxes are decided by the app.
  2. Goals. Add as many as you need:
    • Survive: an opponent, its move, its attack points and boost, and the minimum chance you want to survive. “I also take the hit above, same turn” adds two attacks in the same turn.
    • Get a KO: your move against an opponent whose HP, Def and SpD points you set. “HP left %” lets you ask for a partial KO (for example 50%). Tick “Count Helping Hand” if your ally will help.
    • Speed: outspeed an opponent, or stay slower than it (Trick Room).
    Each goal has its own “Field & weather” box: weather, terrain, Tailwind, Reflect, Light Screen, Aurora Veil, Friend Guard and Helping Hand, separately for your side and the opponent’s. “Auto” reads the weather and terrain from the abilities on the field (if both sides set one, the slower Pokémon’s wins, as in battle).
  3. Priority. If not everything fits in 66 points, EVSmith drops the “Nice to have” goals first, then “Important”, and keeps “Required” as long as it can.
  4. Tournament meta (optional). Adds goals built from the most used Pokémon in tournaments: surviving their best attacks and/or outspeeding them. Your own goals always come first; the meta only uses the extra points you allow. The button “Use the most used tournament set” in a goal fills ability, item, move and nature from tournament data.
  5. Press Find the best spread.

Reading the result

  • The coloured bar and the six boxes are the spread. “Free points” are the ones no goal needs; you are not forced to spend them, and a suggestion is given.
  • Each goal shows ✅ (met), ❌ (not met) or 🤝 (met only with Helping Hand), the damage range in % of HP and the chance of reaching the threshold across the 16 damage rolls.
  • Why these points? lists, for every stat, the goals that need its last point (the goal is met with it and fails with one point less). If no single goal pins a stat, its points are a trade-off between HP, Def and SpD, or come from the extra points.
  • For KO goals EVSmith also shows the minimum Atk/SpA points without and with Helping Hand, so you can see how many points the boost saves.
  • “Other natures” lists the runner-up natures and whether they meet fewer goals.

Saving and sharing

  • Your Pokémon, goals and options are saved automatically in this browser and come back when you reopen the page.
  • Copy link puts your whole setup in a link you can send to someone; opening it loads the same goals. Reset clears everything.
  • Under each result, Copy set gives text you can paste in Pokémon Showdown (Teambuilder → Import). The Stat Points go in the EVs line, which is how Showdown’s Champions format reads them. The moves are the ones from your KO goals.

Good to know

  • The “Where should leftover points go?” list has a Resistance mode: it searches every HP/Def/SpD split of the leftover points and keeps the one with the best average HP margin against the attacks in your Survive goals (and the tournament meta, if on).
  • Extra HP optimizations (section 1) tune the HP stat with leftover points: multiples of 16 for Leftovers-type recovery, 4n for Sitrus Berry, 10n-1 for Life Orb, 4n+1 for Substitute and so on. Max HP and Min HP choose between equal options. Your goals are never weakened.
  • Level 50, doubles, IVs 31. Spread moves are reduced by 25% (two targets). Critical hits are optional per goal.
  • On multi-hit moves, Multiscale and damage-reducing berries only work on the first hit.
  • Weather and terrain in “Auto” come only from abilities that set them when the Pokémon enters (Drought, Drizzle, Sand Stream, Snow Warning and the Surges). Sand Spit and Seed Sower trigger only after being hit, so choose the weather or terrain by hand if you want to assume they are active.
  • Abilities that depend on the situation (Blaze, Torrent, Overgrow, Swarm, Flash Fire, Stakeout, Electromorphosis, Supreme Overlord) do nothing until you switch them on: your Pokémon’s in section 1, the opponent’s inside a Survive goal. Merciless makes every hit a critical hit against a poisoned target automatically.
  • Foul Play uses your Atk. In a Survive goal against it, EVSmith tries each Atk value your KO goals need and keeps the best combination, and it will not add extra Atk points that would break that goal.
  • Not supported: Counter, Mirror Coat, Metal Burst, Flail, Reversal, Fling, one-hit-KO moves, moves that depend on turn order, and the abilities Disguise and Imposter.
  • Results are estimates for planning. Always test a spread in battle.

Example

You want Incineroar to survive a Wood Hammer from a Rillaboom with 32 Attack points, and to KO that Rillaboom with Flare Blitz. Pick Incineroar, add a “Survive” goal (Rillaboom, Wood Hammer, 32 attack points, 100%) and a “Get a KO” goal (Flare Blitz on Rillaboom), then press the button. EVSmith returns the fewest points that do both, and what is left over.

Extra HP optimizations

Spends leftover points on HP so that your HP stat fits what you tick (n is any whole number). It never takes points from your goals.

Import a Pokémon Showdown set

Paste a set (Teambuilder → Export). The Pokémon, ability, item, nature and EVs are filled in; the EVs are read as Stat Points (classic 0–252 EVs are divided by 8).

2What must it do?

3Tournament meta (Reg M-C)

Two independent options: turn on one, the other, or both.

The meta is added to your own goals: your goals come first, then the meta uses the points you allow. Each attack counts by how many teams use the Pokémon, how high the attack is in its list, and how often it hits (accuracy). Tournament results do not show Stat Points, so the points are assumptions.

All Pokémon and their tournament data

Blank means I have no certain data. Usage = share of tournament teams (online events, last 30 days, Reg M-C). Abilities, items and moves come from tournament results, most used first; percentages are shown where I have them.
